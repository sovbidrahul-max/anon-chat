The Artist

Creating from the soul, not the mind

My work lives at the intersection of raw emotion and deliberate technique. Each canvas is a conversation — between colour and silence, between what is seen and what is felt.

Trained at the Royal Academy and influenced by time spent in Morocco, Japan, and the Scottish Highlands, I bring a global sensitivity to deeply personal subjects.

Choose & Purchase
